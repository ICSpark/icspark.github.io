PROGRAMS

All of our programs are free, geared towards 6th - 12th grade students and lead by undergraduate mentors. No prior coding knowledge is required. Parents and students will attend a mandatory orientation at the beginning of the program semester prior to the beginning of classes. Upon registration, we will contact you by email with more details regarding the specific time and place. Below you can find information of our classes.

Spring 2027 Intro to Web Development Course

We are excited to launch our 8th year of ICSpark this upcoming Spring, We are offering both in-person and online options for students! Registration for Spring 2027 will be open from November 16th till December 23rd, 2026. Limited seats are available on a first-sign-up, first-served basis.

Program Details

Who: 6th - 12th grade students, no coding experience required. Classes are led by college students.

What: Intro to Web Development Course

When: January 16th - April 18th, 2027, every Saturday from 9am - 12pm. Signing up is a 4-month commitment, as the course is fast paced. Please sign up only if you know you can commit to attending all sessions. Student-parent orientation and final project showcase date and times will be provided to participants.

Where: In person at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, OR online via Zoom

Why: Great opportunity for students to learn from college mentors who are familiar with the challenges of learning to code. Computer science is an invaluable skill for any field of study.
